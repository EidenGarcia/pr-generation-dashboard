Method

Live status. Each unit in dataSource.js is read as published. Units below 1 MW are “offline”. Operator: Genera PR runs PREPA’s legacy thermal plants (steam, combined cycle, gas turbines, temporary units); AES, EcoEléctrica and renewables are independent producers under PPOAs; hydro stays with PREPA. Primary fuel for dual-fuel plants follows Genera’s latest filing (San Juan CC and Costa Sur steam mostly on LNG).

Live cost. The feed’s unit cost is in ¢/kWh — it lines up with fuel price × heat rate (for example Costa Sur on LNG, Palo Seco on Bunker C). Cost per hour = MW × ¢/kWh × 10. Units with no reported cost (hydro, some peakers) add MW but no cost.

Historical cost. From each filing’s unit sheets: fuel consumed (barrels), heat value (Btu/barrel) and gross/net generation. Oil is priced per barrel; LNG per MMBtu. Prices come from the feed where it publishes one for that plant and fuel; otherwise the average feed ULSD price, or the NFE contract formula for LNG (115% × Henry Hub plus an adder). All months are priced the same way, so changes reflect fuel volumes and mix, not price swings.

Customer charges. FCA and PPCA are PREB’s approved quarterly factors, in ¢ per kWh billed. They cover fuel for all of Genera’s plants plus energy bought from AES, EcoEléctrica and renewables, and they include reconciliations of past quarters and grid losses, so they won’t equal Genera’s fuel cost per kWh generated. Household amounts are those two charges × monthly use.

Not included. Fixed O&M, capacity payments, PPOA energy purchases, fuel logistics and hedging. Q3 of FY 2025-26 (January–March 2026) was not in the uploaded workbooks.

How these pages update

Every hour a GitHub Action reads PREPA’s dataSource.js and dataGraph.js, logs whether the portal answered, records any unit that stopped or restarted, adds a row to the hourly history, rebuilds the 24-hour forecast and commits the files in data/. GitHub Pages publishes the commit and open pages pick it up within five minutes.

Each quarter, add Genera’s new “Fuel Consumption, Generation & Heat Rates” exhibit to the filings data and PREB’s new FCA/PPCA factors to data/customer_fuel_charges.json.

Data: PREPA operational feeds (hourly), Genera quarterly filings in PREB docket NEPR-MI-2019-0007, PREB FCA/PPCA resolutions in NEPR-MI-2020-0001. Estimates are labelled as such; see Sources & method.
